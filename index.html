How to play

Three things to know before you open it

  1. 01
    Tap to flapEvery tap gives the bird one lift. Let go, and gravity pulls it back down.
  2. 02
    Watch the city behind youThe skyline scrolls past as you fly — use it as a sense of speed and distance.
  3. 03
    One hit ends the runClip a pipe or the ground and it's over. Your best score is shown the moment you land.
